Privacy Policy

DAHLAH7 Social Control

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how DAHLAH7 Social Control handles information when you use the local tools to connect your own social-media accounts through official platform APIs.

Designed for local use: API credentials and access tokens are intended to remain on the computer where the tool is installed. This website does not provide a server that receives your social-media tokens or posts on your behalf.

Information accessed

After you authorize a platform, the tool may access the categories of information covered by the permissions you approve. Depending on the platform and granted scopes, this can include account/profile details, your published content, public videos, comments, engagement metrics, and permission to publish or manage content. The tool does not receive permissions you have not granted.

How information is used

Information obtained through an official API is used locally to perform the action you request, such as listing your content, checking account information, reviewing permitted engagement data, or publishing content after you instruct the tool to do so. API requests are sent directly to the relevant platform. The website does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

In the local setup, application configuration and OAuth tokens are stored in files on your computer, in the user's Hermes social-accounts directory. The setup applies restrictive file permissions. Do not share these files, API secrets, authorization codes, or access tokens. Local storage is not a guarantee against compromise of your device; keep the operating system and software updated and protect your user account.

Third-party platforms

Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, and their respective API providers process information under their own privacy terms and platform policies. When you authorize an account or publish content, you are interacting with that platform. Review the relevant platform policy and revoke permissions there when you no longer want the tool to access your account.

Retention and deletion

You control the local configuration and token files. To stop API access, revoke the application in the relevant platform's security or connected-app settings and remove the local token/configuration files for the integration. Content already published to a social platform remains subject to that platform's controls and retention rules; deleting local files does not delete published posts.

Website logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process routine technical request data (for example, IP address, browser type, and request time) for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. The social-media API helper itself is intended to communicate directly with the platform APIs from your device.

Children

This service is not designed for children who are below the minimum age required by the relevant social-media platform.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the local tool's functionality or data handling changes. The effective date at the top indicates the latest published version.
